# Boutiqly social brand

Copied from the Boutiqly project (`claude/boutiqly-social-brand.md`) on Oct 3, 2026, as seed material for onboarding Boutiqly as the app's second brand. What Claude follows when writing Boutiqly posts, captions and Threads drafts. Set up Oct 1, 2026 with Katy. Strategy and schedule: `social-strategy.md`; colors: `colors.md`.

## Who's talking
- Two shop owners who built the thing they needed: Katy (Mosaic Makers Collective, Dallas) and Ashley Collett (Salt and Honey Market, Salt Lake City).
- Brand posts say "we" (Katy & Ashley). Founder-story posts are Katy's "I", signed "— Katy".
- Founder-led, not pitchy. Lead with the owner's real problem (the 9pm-at-the-shop moment), then how it gets easier.
- Real faces and real voices. No AI avatars or AI-generated people, ever.

## Voice rules
- Plain, warm, specific. Shop owner to shop owner.
- Never use: empower, thrive, streamline, seamless, elevate, unlock, solution, operating system, flexible plans, unique needs.
- Say "independent shops" or "indie retail". Use "boutiques" only on boutique-specific posts (it leaves out makers, consignment shops and market organizers).
- No made-up proof: no invented savings, stats, hours saved or tester quotes. Use a tester's words only after they've said yes.
- Don't claim Boutiqly calculates or pays out vendor payouts. It doesn't.
- Plan names are public (Katy, Oct 2 2026): Lite, Boutiqly and Plus, and posts can say which plan includes a feature. Prices and trial length: still check boutiqly.io before stating them.

## Per platform
- **Instagram**: warm, emotional, community. Emoji welcome. One clear next step (free plan link in bio).
- **Facebook**: casual, peer operator, the lived problem.
- **LinkedIn**: insight first, peer-to-peer. Light on emoji.
- **Threads**: conversational, lowercase is fine. One real detail, then a question.
- **TikTok / Reels**: founder on camera or real shop footage; hook in the first line.
- **Stories**: short, one sticker or link at most.

## Hashtags (proposed, confirm with Katy)
- Instagram: 3–5, e.g. #independentretail #shopsmall #smallbusinessowner #boutiqueowner (boutique posts only) #makersgonnamake (maker posts only).
- LinkedIn, Threads, Facebook: 0–2.

## Facts Claude may state
- Built by two shop owners who use it every day in their own shops: Katy (Mosaic Makers Collective, two Dallas locations: Bishop Arts and Dallas Farmers Market, 150+ makers) and Ashley (Salt and Honey Market, Salt Lake City).
- For independent shops: boutiques, makers and artisans, consignment shops, pop-up and market organizers.
- One inbox for texts, email, and Instagram and Facebook messages.
- Automations: missed-call text-back, customer follow-ups, review requests, wholesale follow-up after trade shows, staff onboarding.
- Marketing: social planner with approvals, email campaigns and newsletters, landing pages, website chat widget.
- Customer lists for shoppers, leads and wholesale buyers, with tags.
- Keep your register and your books. Boutiqly connects the tools you won't give up; it doesn't replace your POS or accounting.
- Shop owners download the Boutiqly app (Katy, Oct 2 2026). No contracts. Don't call Boutiqly web-based or say there's nothing to download.
- Free Boutiqly Plan assessment: boutiqly.io/boutiqly-plan-assessment.
- Plans (boutiqly.io/features, Oct 2 2026). Every plan: all-in-one inbox, email marketing (groups by purchase history, how often they shop, interests), social planner, reviews. Boutiqly and Plus: automations (welcome, birthday, thank-you, win-back), team hiring and management (role-based logins). Plus: courses and memberships, analytics and reporting. Add-ons: consignment management, market management (applications, booth assignments, booth payments).
- The features page says consignment does "payout calculations". Don't repeat it: Katy confirmed Oct 2 2026 to keep payouts out of posts.
- Lines Katy uses: "Built by the owners who run them." · "You're great at running your shop. You're drowning in everything else." · "We didn't make software for shop owners. We made it as shop owners."

## Photo rules
- Katy and Ashley: faces are fine.
- Tester shop owners, staff and customers: only after they've said yes. Until then, hands, products and shop interiors.
- Never show the face of anyone under 18.
- Boutiqly screenshots: use demo data, or blur customer names, phone numbers, emails and message text.

## Look
- Colors (Katy chose the current palette over the 2024 guide, Oct 1 2026): Deep Forest #1d3c34, Green #276f3d, Orange #de771f, Sage #99cc99, Light Blue #a1cfd6, Pale Mint #c4e5e2, Pink #e9c0d1. Neutrals: Cream #fbf8f3, White, Muted #5b6f69.
- The 2024 brand guide's Marine #276F5D, rust "Earth" and Lilac #D595C2 are retired. Swap them for Green, Orange and Pink when reusing older Canva pages.
- Orange is for big bold text and buttons only (too light for small text on white or cream).
- Font: Proxima Nova (Bold headlines, Semibold caps tagline, Medium body); Montserrat is the free stand-in.
- Logo kit (2024 guide by Ashley Collett Design): hanger logo with "Retail made simple", secondary wordmark, stacked BTQLY logo, hanger icon and B icon, in color and reversed. Multicolor wordmark = each letter a brand color.
- Brand guide extras worth using in graphics: 11 retail line icons (hanger, register, cart, price tag, sale burst, storefront, bags, growth chart) and the hanger and B-tile patterns.
- Plain studio look: no checkerboard strips or burst shapes.
- Keep the voice rules above even where the 2024 guide's mission copy uses "empower", "thrive" or "streamline".

## Post look (first sample batch, Oct 1 2026; Katy to react)
- Matches Katy's own Boutiqly videos: Cream, Pale Mint or Pink-tint backgrounds with soft blurred color blobs; Deep Forest panels for dark slides.
- Montserrat ExtraBold headlines: line 1 in Deep Forest, line 2 in Green (Sage on dark). Body in Montserrat Medium, Muted or Deep Forest.
- Small caps label pills (Deep Forest on Mint, or Mint on Forest). The orange "Get your free plan" pill is the only orange text (white, bold, 40px+).
- Single-color hanger logo (Deep Forest on light, Mint on dark), cut from the brand guide. The multicolor wordmark isn't recolored.
- Brand-guide line icons (register, growth chart, hanger) for diagrams. App screens come from Katy's feature videos (demo data only).
- Reels: 9:16, silent AAC track so Katy adds trending audio on her phone; captions in white rounded boxes; the logo sits on a dark top fade; end card with the orange plan pill.
- Stories: keep text between y 250 and 1580. Leave clear space for link, poll and question stickers, and say where in the piece note.
- Second batch (Oct 1 2026, community and encouragement): shareable formats in the same palette: ticket-style permission slips, a bingo card, a receipt, chat-bubble scripts, a tear-off calendar, kinetic-text Reels over blurred shop photos. Accent fonts only as props: Caveat for signatures, Space Mono for the receipt.
- Shareable carousels carry the logo on every slide so reposts keep the credit. Repost cards meant for owners' own shoppers keep the logo small and muted, or leave it off: Katy had it removed from the shop-small card (Oct 2 2026), so ask before adding one to the next repost card.
- Story library (Oct 1 2026): Katy asked for "a variety of brand colors, shapes, and elements," so Stories go past the plain look: arches, bold circles, rings, scallop badges, hanging signs, tickets, polaroid frames, a hanger pattern, wavy lines, stripes and sparkles, on any palette color (Orange backgrounds carry only big bold text). Still no checkerboard. Story text stays between y 250 and 1600; sticker space is left clear and described in the note.
- Story graphics rules (after Katy's Oct 2 2026 review): shapes are drawn smooth (supersampled), arrows are smooth curves with the head lined up with the stroke, and decorations are deliberate, never scattered: confetti is a dotted garland on three arcs in two opposite corners, dots are a halftone grid fading from a corner, sparkles come as a set of three in a corner. Corner stripes run all the way from one edge to the other, never stopping mid-canvas (Katy, Oct 2 2026). Decorations stay out of the text area. Content blocks without a sticker are centered between y 270 and 1590. Slider gauges never use a segment color close to the background (it gets swapped for Green or Orange).
- Boutiqly has no shop hours. "We're open" and "Our hours" Stories are about Boutiqly being open to shops and there whenever owners are ("Open whenever you are"); the "Our hours" and "Save the date" templates are blank for Katy to fill in.
- Story threads (Oct 2 2026): Katy asked for three multi-frame Stories posted all at once: FAQs (7 frames), Features (15) and Our story (12), reusing the library frames. Each opens on a cover (a numbered contents card, or the two founder photos) with a handwritten "tap through" cue and a right-pointing arrow, and ends on a free-plan frame carrying the thread's only Link sticker. Neighboring frames alternate light and dark backgrounds.
- Feature batch (Oct 2 2026): one post or carousel plus one Reel per feature on boutiqly.io/features. Reels mix three styles: animated graphics, the feature videos' app footage reframed to 9:16 under brand headlines (scenes synced to the video's own headlines), and photo Reels. Feature copy comes from the feature videos' own on-screen headlines; anything about texting carries the texting-fees note.
- Gentle content (Katy, Oct 3 2026: "it all feels salesy"): keep a steady share of posts with no pitch at all: encouragement, retail tips owners can use the same day, and community prompts. These carry the logo but no free-plan pill or line, no feature names and no "Boutiqly" in the caption. Their end cards are logo only. Don't make up stories about Katy or Ashley to fill them; general shop-owner moments are fine.
