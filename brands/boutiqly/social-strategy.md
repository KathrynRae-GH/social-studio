## Weekly minimums (what Katy can approve, not targets)
- Feed (Instagram + Facebook): 3 a week, Mon / Wed / Fri. 1 of the 3 is a Reel. Tue / Thu take extras when there's more to share.

- TikTok: none until there's an account (then 1 a week, the week's Reel).

## Best times (starting points, Central time)
Boutiqly posted 9 times Jul–Sep 2026 (Boutiqly social planner stats: 8 Instagram, 1 Facebook), too few to read patterns from. These come from 2026 platform-wide studies, in local time. Re-run from the planner's stats after ~8 weeks at the new cadence.

| Platform | Days | Time | Why |
|---|---|---|---|

Sources: Sprout Social, "Best Times to Post on Social Media 2026" (updated Mar 31, 2026; ~2B engagements, 307K profiles) · Buffer, "Best Time to Post on Social Media in 2026" (updated Mar 25, 2026).

## Content pillars (rotate; no two the same back-to-back)
Katy, Oct 1 2026: the first batch leaned too far into Boutiqly sales. Spread it out with encouragement for shop owners and artisans, and engaging, shareable posts.
1. **Encouragement**: permission slips, Monday notes, hot takes that take the pressure off. Made to be sent to a friend.
2. **Community**: games (bingo), fill-in-the-blanks, this-or-that polls, shop humor, repost cards owners can share with their own shoppers. Made for comments and shares.
3. **Shop-owner tips**: practical how-tos: follow-ups, reply scripts, seasonal plans (Small Business Saturday is Nov 28, 2026).
4. **Behind the build**: the founders' shops, the founding testers, building V2, real numbers Katy is willing to share.
5. **Boutiqly**: features in real life (inbox, automations, planner) and offers (free Boutiqly Plan assessment, trial, white glove, tester stories with their OK).
